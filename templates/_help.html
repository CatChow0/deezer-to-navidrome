Downloads are built in

This app now downloads from Deezer by itself. Here is what changed, and how to use it.

Downloads from 1.10.0 to 1.11.0 may be damaged

Those versions decrypted the files wrongly: they play with noise and skips, and Navidrome's web player refuses them. Downloads are correct again. Repair downloads checks every file saved since 1.10.0 against Deezer and replaces only the damaged ones, keeping their tags; then refresh the library.

What changed

  • Download anything from the app. A track, a whole album, or an artist's discography, from search, a playlist, or the Releases view. Files are tagged with the cover included.
  • See where each download is. The button shows Queued, then 42% or 3/14, then Downloaded. The Activity cell shows the details.
  • Retry what failed. When some tracks of an album fail, the button shows 13/14 saved: click it again to fetch only the missing ones.
  • Quality follows your account. Premium downloads FLAC, a free account MP3 128 kbps. With a free account, add the arl of a premium one in Settings for the downloads only: your mixes keep using the first.
  • Explicit stays explicit. Explicit tracks and albums are marked E, and you get exactly the version you clicked. When a release is not available in your account's country, the app may take another release of the same recording, but never the censored one in place of the explicit one.
  • Your account's country. Settings → Deezer account shows the country next to the account badge: Deezer serves the catalogue of that country, which decides what can be downloaded.
  • New setting. Settings → Performance → Download threads sets how many downloads run at the same time.

Reopen this window anytime with Help in the top bar.

Getting started

  1. Settings. Check the music folders and the Navidrome paths, make sure the line under Downloads is green, and connect your Deezer account.
  2. Index your library. Settings → Library index → Rebuild from scratch the first time; afterwards Refresh library only reads new files.
  3. Add playlists. Paste a Deezer playlist link in Playlists, or follow your Flow and daily mixes with Mixes → Choose mixes.
  4. Scan. Open a playlist and run a Quick scan (only re-checks what is missing) or a Full scan. It writes the .m3u8 Navidrome imports.
  5. Download what is missing. Download missing in the playlist, or Download on a single row. Then Refresh library and a Quick scan so the new files count as In library.
  6. Automate. Automation in the playlist scans it on a schedule and can download what is missing each time.

If something goes wrong

A download fails
Hover the Retry button or look in Activity for the reason. "Not available for your Deezer account" means the track is not in your account's catalogue: its country is shown in Settings → Deezer account.
"Only the censored version is available"
The explicit version is not in your account's catalogue (its country is shown in Settings → Deezer account). The app refuses to save the censored one in its place.
Only MP3 128 kbps
The account is free. Add a premium account's arl in Settings → Deezer account.
Downloaded tracks still show as Missing
Run Refresh library, then a Quick scan of the playlist.
A track matched the wrong file
Click Fix on its row and pick the right file, or choose Not in my library.
The arl stopped working
Deezer revokes it when you sign out of deezer.com. Copy a fresh one.
