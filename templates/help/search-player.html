Search and player

Finding music

Find music on Deezer on the Dashboard searches tracks, albums and artists. Open an album or an artist for its details. Clear empties the search.

What you already own

TracksOwned when the file is in your library
Albumsowned / total tracks, amber when partial, green when complete
Artistsowned / total albums

These read your library index, so run Refresh library after adding files. An E marks explicit tracks and albums.

Previews and the player

The play button plays a 30-second Deezer preview, with the other results queued after it. The player's volume button also has Level out loudness, which brings every preview to a similar loudness using Deezer's measure of each track.

Listen in full opens the official Deezer player. It uses your browser's own Deezer login, so what plays in full depends on that account, not on the one in Settings.
