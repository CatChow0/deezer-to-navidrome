Spotify and CSV

Importing

Each track of an imported playlist is matched to a Deezer track. From then on it works like a Deezer playlist: previews, Download missing, scans, the .m3u8 and automation.

FromHowLimits

CSV fileImport CSV, with a file from Exportify, TuneMyMusic or SoundiizNone: any size, private playlists too

Use a CSV for a playlist over 100 tracks. It usually carries each track's ISRC, which gives an exact match.

What the statuses mean

Picked by handChosen with Find on Deezer, and kept by every later sync

Correcting a match

The To review filter lists the tracks to check. Find on Deezer on a row searches Deezer with the source's artist and title, and previews each result. Choose Use this track, Not on Deezer, or Back to automatic matching.

An explicit track is never traded for its censored version silently.

Keeping it up to date

  • A Quick scan reads the Spotify playlist again and matches the tracks added since.
  • A Full scan also matches again every track not picked by hand.
  • With Automation on, new tracks are matched and can be downloaded on schedule.
  • A CSV import is a snapshot: import the file again to update it.
