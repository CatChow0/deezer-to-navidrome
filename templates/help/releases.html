Releases

The release feed

The Releases tab lists recent albums, EPs and singles of your favorite Deezer artists, with what you already own. They are the favorites of this profile's Deezer account, the default one unless its Navidrome account chose another; two profiles on the same Deezer account share the feed and its muted artists.

It refreshes on the interval set in Settings → Release feed, from every 6 hours to once a week, or never. Refresh now always works.

Choosing what you see

  • The buttons at the top filter by Albums or EPs and singles.
  • Hide features hides releases where your artist is only a guest.
  • Hide what I own hides what is already in your library.
  • Mute in Artists you follow hides an artist's releases, and Unmute brings them back.
