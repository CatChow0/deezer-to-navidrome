Stats and recaps

Where your plays come from

The app keeps its own history of what you play. New plays are sent by Navidrome as they happen, and past ones can be imported from Navidrome and Maloja. Neither Maloja nor lb-enricher is needed, and the app needs no Navidrome password.

One history per profile

Each Navidrome account has its own history, its own token and its own recaps: the Stats tab shows those of the profile chosen at the top of the page. A rebuild only takes the plays of the profile's Navidrome user in Navidrome's database, the user found by Test in Settings → Navidrome accounts, else the one with its user name. Maloja kept one person's plays, so only the account made from your install reads it.

Your install's history stays where it was. A removed account's history is kept in backups/removed-accounts/. Made for you still reads your install's history for now.

Recording new plays

  1. Create a token in Settings → Listening stats, or set SCROBBLE_TOKEN. Each profile has its own: choose the profile at the top of the page first.
  2. Set ND_LISTENBRAINZ_BASEURL=http://deezer-to-navidrome:8080/apis/listenbrainz/1/ in Navidrome and restart it, once for all its users. Change the host if Navidrome reaches this app under another name.
  3. Signed in to Navidrome as that profile's user, link ListenBrainz in the personal settings using its token. If Navidrome already holds a token, you can paste that one in Settings instead.
  4. Play a track and find it under Stats → Recent plays.

The tokens are checked even with DISABLE_AUTH, and two profiles cannot share one: the token says whose play it is. Making a new one stops that user's plays until it is linked again.

Importing your past plays

Mount the sources read-only, then choose Rebuild (merge) under Stats → Your history (Manage history jumps there):

volumes:
  - /path/to/navidrome/data:/import/navidrome:ro
  - /path/to/Maloja:/import/maloja:ro

The app reads copies of these files and never writes to them. When the same play is in both, Navidrome's details win, and it counts once even if Maloja changed the title or its clock was a few minutes off.

Merge can be run at any time without counting anything twice, and also records each track's exact file. Start clean says how many plays it would lose before you confirm, and refuses to run if Navidrome's database has no plays.

The first import saves a copy of Maloja's database, so rebuilds keep working after you remove Maloja. Keep the Navidrome mount: Start clean needs it, and Merge reads new Navidrome plays through it.

Going back to a backup

Both rebuilds first keep a backup of the profile's history (the last 3, as stats.db.bak-* in the data folder for your install's account, stats/nd2.db.bak-*… for the others). To restore one, stop the container, delete the history and its -wal and -shm files, rename the backup to its name, and start it again.

Reset data in Settings does not touch your listening history.

Recaps

Each month and year gets a story of up to 12 slides that move on by themselves. The month in progress is marked so far.

Next or previous slideTap the right or left side, swipe, or use the arrow keys or on-screen arrows
PauseHold on the slide, press Space, or use Pause
CloseEscape or the × button

The slides depend on what stood out in that period, so they differ from one recap to the next. Your listener personality compares the period with your usual habits, so a night owl only earns that title in an unusually late month.

Comparisons with the previous period are left out when that period is only partly recorded, and "new to you" waits until 90 days of history exist, since your records start when recording started.

Recap sound

Recaps play your own files, starting about a third of the way into each song, using the music folders already mounted here. A track with no playable file uses Deezer's 30-second preview, and otherwise the slide stays silent.

Covers

Covers are stored in the data folder. Tracks and albums use art inside your files, then a cover, folder or front image in the album's own folder, then Deezer. Artists use an artist or folder image in their folder, then their Deezer photo, then their most-played album's cover.

They fill in the background, most played first, and a cover is also fetched the first time a page needs it.

How the numbers are worked out

  • Listening time is plays × track length, so it is an estimate, not measured listening. A note appears when some lengths are unknown.
  • A featured artist counts for each credited artist, while the play counts once in the total.
  • Genres come from your files and then Deezer, a few tracks at a time. Similar names are grouped, like Rap and Hip Hop. A note says how many plays have a known genre.
  • Periods follow your browser's timezone.
