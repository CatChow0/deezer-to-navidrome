Troubleshooting

A download fails

  1. Settings → Deezer accounts must list an account, and the line under the list names the one used for downloads. Without an account, nothing can be downloaded.
  2. Settings → Downloads must show a green line. A red one means the folder does not exist or cannot be written to: mount it as a writable volume.
  3. The reason of each failure is in Activity and in the tooltip of the Retry button.

"Not available for your Deezer account" or "no download link for this track" usually means the track is not in your account's catalogue. Its country is shown in Settings → Deezer accounts.

"Only the censored version is available"

The explicit version is not in your account's catalogue. The app refuses to save the censored one in its place.

Only MP3 128 kbps

The account used for downloads is free. Add a premium account in Settings → Deezer accounts and mark it Use for download to get FLAC.

The arl stopped working

Deezer session cookies expire, and are revoked when you sign out of deezer.com. Copy a fresh one.

Downloaded files play with noise, skip, or don't play in Navidrome

Versions 1.10.0 to 1.11.0 decrypted downloads wrongly. Repair downloads checks every file saved since 1.10.0 against Deezer and replaces only the damaged ones, keeping their tags. Files that are fine, and files it cannot identify on Deezer, are left as they are. Then run Refresh library.

Downloaded tracks still show as Missing

The library index does not know the new files yet. Run Refresh library, then a Quick scan of the playlist. With automation, the next automatic scan picks them up.

Downloaded tracks are missing from Navidrome's playlists

The downloads folder needs a mapping in Settings → Navidrome paths, and Navidrome must see that folder too.

A track matched the wrong file

Click Fix on its row and pick the right file, or choose Not in my library.

No matches are found

  • Tags. Files lack artist and title tags, and their names do not carry them either.
  • Artist names. The artist must match closely: fix the odd ones with Fix.
  • Wrong folders. The music folders are empty, not mounted, or the index was never built.

Playlists are not written

  • The library index is empty: build it first.
  • A mapping is wrong: check Settings → Navidrome paths.
  • The generated playlists folder cannot be written to.
docker logs deezer-to-navidrome
docker exec deezer-to-navidrome ls -la /music

Scans or matching are slow

  • Set cpus in docker-compose.yml to your real core count.
  • Match the scan threads to your storage, and the matching threads to your CPU count.
  • Prefer Refresh library to a rebuild, and Quick scan to Full scan.

Duplicates keep coming back, or settings are lost

The data folder is not persistent. Mount it as a volume:

volumes:
  - ./data:/data

Disk space keeps growing

Quarantined duplicates are still on disk. See Library and duplicates to empty the folder.

New plays don't appear in Stats

  1. Check that ND_LISTENBRAINZ_BASEURL points at this app, and that Navidrome was restarted.
  2. Check that a token exists in Settings → Listening stats, and that Navidrome holds the same one.
  3. Play a track long enough for Navidrome to count it, then look under Stats → Recent plays.
  4. Look at the logs of this app: a request answered 401 means the tokens differ, and 400 means the request was not understood. No request at all means Navidrome cannot reach this address.

Plays made in the meantime are not lost as long as Navidrome keeps them: Rebuild (merge) picks them up.

Stats is empty

Nothing has been imported or recorded yet. Use Rebuild (merge) under Your history with the sources mounted, or set up new plays. See Stats and recaps.

Rebuild (merge) or Start clean is greyed out

Your history shows whether each source was found. Merge needs at least one source, or the saved copy of Maloja. Start clean needs Navidrome's database to be mounted, because most of your history could not be rebuilt without it.

Covers are missing

Right after an import they are still being found: covers fill in the background, most played first, which can take about an hour on a large library. Your history says how many tracks are still being checked on Deezer. A track with no art in its file and no match on Deezer shows its initials.

After upgrading from an earlier build, run Rebuild (merge) once, so albums can use the art in your files.

A recap has no sound

  • Some browsers block sound until you interact with the page: tap once on the recap.
  • Check Mute and the volume at the top of the recap.
  • A track with no playable file falls back on a Deezer preview. With neither, the slide stays silent.

Listening time looks too low, or genres look incomplete

Time only counts tracks whose length is known, and notes on the Stats tab and in recaps say what share is covered. Genres fill in as Deezer is checked. Run Rebuild (merge) if you imported before adding a source.

A number in Stats looks wrong

Every play can be checked under Stats → Recent plays, with its time, title, artist and album. Opening an artist, album or track shows its plays by month.

The version shows "unknown"

The VERSION file is missing from the image. Pull it again:

docker compose pull && docker compose up -d
