Playlists and mixes

Adding a playlist

On the Dashboard, paste a Deezer playlist link or ID in Playlists. Spotify links and CSV files work too: see Spotify and CSV.

Mixes

Mixes → Choose mixes lists the Flow and the mixes Deezer makes for the Deezer account of this profile, the default one unless its Navidrome account chose another, and lets you follow them. Their tracks change, and every scan picks up the current ones, read with that account.

Quick scan or Full scan

ButtonWhat it doesWhen to use it
Quick scanKeeps the tracks already in your library and only checks the missing and unchecked ones againMost of the time
Full scanMatches every track againAfter reorganizing your library

Both write the playlist's .m3u8, report.json and missing.txt, in its own folder under the generated playlists folder.

What the track statuses mean

In libraryA file of yours was matched to this track
MissingNo file matched. It can be downloaded
Not checkedNo scan has looked at this track yet

The buttons above the list filter by status.

Fixing a wrong match

Click Fix on a row to search your library, then Use this file, or choose Not in my library. Back to automatic matching undoes it. Your choice stays through later scans.

Getting playlists into Navidrome

The app and Navidrome usually see the same files under different paths. Settings → Navidrome paths translates them, so the .m3u8 points where Navidrome looks. For example, the app sees /music/deezer-dl/songs/Song.mp3 and Navidrome sees /deezer-dl/songs/Song.mp3.

Both containers must see the same files, and the downloads folder needs a mapping too, or downloaded tracks are left out of the playlists.

A .m3u8 imported by Navidrome always belongs to its first admin. To give each Navidrome user their own playlists, add their accounts in Settings → Navidrome accounts: see below.

Playlists for several Navidrome users

Each playlist goes to the Navidrome accounts ticked under In Navidrome in its drawer. For each one, the app makes a private playlist as that user through Navidrome's API and replaces its content at each update, in place, so it keeps its place in every app. A change made to it in Navidrome is lost at the next update.

  • Tracks are found by their path in Navidrome's database, mounted at /import/navidrome. Without it, by their tags, which misses some. A track Navidrome has not scanned yet is added once it has: the app tries again every 10 minutes.
  • Per account. A playlist added goes to the account of this browser, and the dashboard shows that account's playlists. Adding one another account already has shares it. Delete takes a shared playlist away from this account only.
  • Your install's account keeps its .m3u8 files until Publish as this user… in Settings: the app publishes its playlists through the API, then removes its own files and the copies Navidrome had imported from them.
  • Only the app's own playlists are ever changed or removed in Navidrome. An admin also sees the private playlists of the other users: those are never touched.
  • In step with Navidrome. A playlist of the app that its user deletes in Navidrome leaves that profile, and the app when no other profile has it. One renamed there keeps its new name, here too. Its tracks still follow the app. This is checked every 10 minutes and when the dashboard opens.
  • Made by hand. Also in Navidrome, under the playlists, shows those the user made in Navidrome, read-only: open one to see its tracks, change it in Navidrome.

Automation

In a playlist, Automation turns on a scan at an interval of minutes, hours or days, and can download the missing tracks after each scan. The library index is refreshed after these scans, and tracks downloaded during one run are picked up by the next. The playlist tile shows the time until the next scan.
