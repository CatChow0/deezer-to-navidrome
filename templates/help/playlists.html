Playlists and mixes

Adding a playlist

On the Dashboard, paste a Deezer playlist link or ID in Playlists. Spotify links and CSV files work too: see Spotify and CSV.

Mixes

With a connected Deezer account, Mixes → Choose mixes lets you follow your Flow and the mixes Deezer makes for you. Their tracks change, and every scan picks up the current ones.

Quick scan or Full scan

ButtonWhat it doesWhen to use it
Quick scanKeeps the tracks already in your library and only checks the missing and unchecked ones againMost of the time
Full scanMatches every track againAfter reorganizing your library

Both write the playlist's .m3u8, report.json and missing.txt, in its own folder under the generated playlists folder.

What the track statuses mean

In libraryA file of yours was matched to this track
MissingNo file matched. It can be downloaded
Not checkedNo scan has looked at this track yet

The buttons above the list filter by status.

Fixing a wrong match

Click Fix on a row to search your library, then Use this file, or choose Not in my library. Back to automatic matching undoes it. Your choice stays through later scans.

Getting playlists into Navidrome

The app and Navidrome usually see the same files under different paths. Settings → Navidrome paths translates them, so the .m3u8 points where Navidrome looks. For example, the app sees /music/deezer-dl/songs/Song.mp3 and Navidrome sees /deezer-dl/songs/Song.mp3.

Both containers must see the same files, and the downloads folder needs a mapping too, or downloaded tracks are left out of the playlists.

Automation

In a playlist, Automation turns on a scan at an interval of minutes, hours or days, and can download the missing tracks after each scan. The library index is refreshed after these scans, and tracks downloaded during one run are picked up by the next. The playlist tile shows the time until the next scan.
