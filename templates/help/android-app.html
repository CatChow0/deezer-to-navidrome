Android app

Installing it

Each version of this server carries its Android app. On your phone, open this page signed in, then Settings → Android app → Download the app, and open the file. Android asks once to allow installs from your browser: allow it, then install.

Settings says so when an image was built without the app, for a fork built without the signing key, say.

Signing in

  1. Enter this server's address, as you open it in a browser.
  2. Sign in with your account. The phone's name, its model by default, is how it appears in Settings → Sign-in and other apps, where you can sign it out.
  3. Pick the profile at the top: the Navidrome account the app shows, as the selector at the top of this page does.

An http:// address works, with a warning: use it only on your local network or Tailscale, since your password and data then travel in clear.

Behind Authelia, or another login proxy

The app cannot get through a login page. Let ^/api/v1/ through without it, as the README shows for Authelia: the phone's token protects those routes. Otherwise the app says the address does not answer as Deezer → Navidrome.

What it does

Everything this page does, for the profile picked at the top: Dashboard (your playlists, adding one, the search), Made for you, Stats, Releases, and under More the Settings, Activity, What's new and this help. Sharing a Deezer or Spotify link to the app from another app opens Add a playlist with it.

  • A scan, a download or any other task shows a message with View, which opens it in Activity. The pulse icon at the top counts what is running.
  • In Made for you, swipe a track right to keep it, left to remove it. With TalkBack, the same choices are in the track's actions.

Without the server

When the server cannot be reached, a banner says so and the app shows the pages you opened before, as they were then, for the same profile only. Nothing is changed meanwhile: the buttons wait for the server. Retry in the banner checks again.

Updates

When this server has a newer app, after an update of the server, the app shows a banner: Update downloads it, checks it, and opens Android's installer. The first time, Android asks to allow installs from D2N. The app keeps its sign-in and its profile. More → Check for updates asks at once.
