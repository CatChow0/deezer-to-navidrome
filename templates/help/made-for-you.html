How a mix is built

Each day the app picks up to 4 seeds among what keeps coming back in your listening: at least a track, an album and an artist, never one artist twice, and not the same seed as the day before.

Around a trackTracks with a close tempo and the same genre, 2 at most per artist
Around an albumThe same, with the artists on the album first, guests included
All about an artistThat artist only: what you play, and what you barely played

About two thirds are tracks you come back to, and one third tracks you own and barely played. Versions of one song, like a live take, count once, and skits and interludes stay out unless you really play them. A mix can be shorter than 30 tracks rather than include tracks that do not belong.

Fix missing metadata

Almost no file carries its tempo, and many have no genre. Fix missing metadata, in Your library or Settings → Library index, finds what each file lacks: the tempo is measured from the audio, the genre comes from Deezer, one search per album. Both are added to the file's tags, so Navidrome sees them too.

  • Your files are safe. A value already there is never replaced. Tags are written on a copy, which replaces the file only if its audio bytes and its other tags are found identical, and if nothing else changed the file meanwhile. If the app is stopped brutally, a file is either the old one or the new one, both complete.
  • Formats. FLAC, MP3, OGG and Opus take tags. Other formats, like WAV and M4A, keep their results in the app.
  • Undo. Undo metadata fix in Manage removes what the app added, except values changed since, and stops writing. Results stay in the app, so the mixes carry on. Fix missing metadata turns writing back on.
  • Each file is done once. Results are kept in the file and in the app's data, and the pass resumes after a restart. A file is looked at again only if it changes. A genre Deezer did not have is tried again after a month.
  • Speed. Settings → Performance → Fix missing metadata threads sets how many files are handled at once. Genre searches stay one at a time, because of Deezer's limit, so the first pass cannot go below their time.
  • Cancel. Cancel in Activity stops it after the files in progress. What is done stays done, and it does not restart on its own until you click the button or turn on a version. It then goes on from where it stopped.
  • When it runs. Nothing touches your files until you click it or turn on a mix. After that, new files are handled on their own. Automatic mixes wait until most of the library is done.
  • Read-only folders. A file that cannot be written still counts for the mixes. Manage says how many there are, and writing is tried again every day, without measuring again.
  • Genres are per album. Deezer gives the genre of an album, so one single can be mislabelled.

After the first pass, Navidrome reads the changed files again at its next scan.

An album's tempo is not its average: a candidate is compared with each track, weighted by your plays. A rarely played track that stands out barely counts, and an album with two tempos keeps both. A tempo and its double, like 90 and 180, count as the same.

A track without a known tempo is not excluded. A track without a known genre only joins mixes around its own artists.

Quarantine

Tracks these playlists download go to the trial folder inside your downloads folder, so Navidrome can play them. The On trial playlist has all of them, and Daily Mix+ replays unheard ones before fetching more.

KeptPlayed twice, starred or rated 4-5 in Navidrome, added to one of your playlists, or also in your library. It moves to songs
To review30 days without any of that, or rated 1-2. It stays playable until you decide
TrashYou chose Remove. Restorable for 30 days, then the file is deleted and the track is never proposed again

The trash is the only thing deleted automatically. The app only ever moves or deletes files in the trial folder and its trash, at /data/trial_trash/.

Limits

Daily Mix+ fetches 10 new tracks per refresh in all, and Discover 30 per week. Downloads stop at 100 unheard tracks or 20 GB in quarantine, and Activity says so. All of this can be changed in Manage. At these defaults in FLAC, quarantine can reach about 14 GB.

When something looks wrong

  • No mix appears. Fix missing metadata may still be running: see Manage, or use Regenerate now. A library or listening history with too little in it gives fewer mixes, or none.
  • A track in the wrong mix. Genres come from your file tags and Deezer, and a wrong tag misleads the mix.
  • "Unmapped path" in Activity. Add the folder in Settings → Navidrome paths. The downloads folder needs a mapping too.
  • Plays do not keep a track. Check that plays reach the app, under Stats → Recent plays.
  • Stars and ratings do nothing. Navidrome's database must be mounted at /import/navidrome. They are read once a day, at the next refresh.
  • "Connect a Deezer account". Daily Mix+ and Discover need one to download.
