The three versions

VersionWhat it holdsRefreshed
Daily MixOnly music you already haveEvery day
Daily Mix+Your music, plus a share of tracks to download, a third by defaultEvery day
DiscoverOnly new releases and tracks you never heard, to downloadEvery week

All are off until you turn them on with Manage. One switch chooses Off, Daily Mix or Daily Mix+, and Discover has its own. Daily Mix needs no Deezer account; the other two download, so they do. Going from one mix to the other keeps the old playlists until the new ones are ready. Off removes them, and tracks on trial stay on trial.

One Made for you per profile

Each Navidrome profile has its own mixes, built from its own listening history (Discover from the releases of its Deezer account) and published for that user only, its own settings and its own quarantine. Manage shows those of the profile chosen at the top of the page. A track on trial is decided with its owner's plays and stars only, and it never shows in another profile's mixes. Mixes are built for one profile after the other.

How a mix is built

At each refresh the app picks up to 4 seeds among what keeps coming back in your listening: at least a track, an album and an artist, never one artist twice, and not the same seed as the last time.

Around a trackTracks with a close tempo and the same genre, 2 at most per artist
Around an albumThe same, with the artists on the album first, guests included
All about an artistThat artist only: what you play, and what you barely played

About two thirds are tracks you come back to, and one third tracks you own and barely played. Versions of one song, like a live take, count once, and skits and interludes stay out unless you really play them. A mix can be shorter than the length you chose rather than include tracks that do not belong.

Fix missing metadata

Almost no file carries its tempo, and many have no genre. Fix missing metadata, in Your library or Settings → Library index, finds what each file lacks: the tempo is measured from the audio, the genre comes from Deezer, one search per album. Both are added to the file's tags, so Navidrome sees them too.

  • Your files are safe. A value already there is never replaced. A FLAC with room in its header only has that header rewritten: it is saved first, so a brutal stop can put it back, and the audio is neither moved nor read. Other files are written on a copy, which replaces them only if its audio bytes and its other tags are found identical, and if nothing else changed the file meanwhile.
  • Formats. FLAC, MP3, OGG and Opus take tags. Other formats, like WAV and M4A, keep their results in the app.
  • Undo. Undo metadata fix in Manage removes what the app added, except values changed since, and stops writing. Results stay in the app, so the mixes carry on. Fix missing metadata turns writing back on.
  • Each file is done once. Results are kept in the file and in the app's data, and the pass resumes after a restart. A file is looked at again only if it changes. A genre Deezer did not have is tried again after a month.
  • Speed. Settings → Performance → Fix missing metadata threads sets how many files are handled at once. A file waiting for its genre steps aside while the others go on, so Deezer's limit of about 8 requests a second does not hold the rest back. The final message in Activity says how long each step took. Stats genre lookups wait until the pass is done, and the speed shown changes as files need Deezer more or less.
  • Cancel. Cancel in Activity stops it after the files in progress. What is done stays done, and it does not restart on its own until you click the button or turn on a version. It then goes on from where it stopped.
  • When it runs. Nothing touches your files until you click it or turn on a mix. After that, new files are handled on their own. Automatic mixes wait until most of the library is done.
  • Read-only folders. A file that cannot be written still counts for the mixes. Manage says how many there are, and writing is tried again every day, without measuring again.
  • Genres are per album. Deezer gives the genre of an album, so one single can be mislabelled.

After the first pass, Navidrome reads the changed files again at its next scan.

An album's tempo is not its average: a candidate is compared with each track, weighted by your plays. A rarely played track that stands out barely counts, and an album with two tempos keeps both. A tempo and its double, like 90 and 180, count as the same.

A track without a known tempo is not excluded. A track without a known genre only joins mixes around its own artists.

Quarantine

Tracks these playlists download go to the trial folder inside your downloads folder, so Navidrome can play them. The On trial playlist has all of them, and Daily Mix+ replays unheard ones before fetching more.

On trialJust downloaded. It stays playable for 30 days by default
To reviewIt earned its place: played twice, starred or rated 4-5 in Navidrome, added to one of your playlists, or also in your library. It waits for Keep, which moves it to songs, as long as needed
TrashIts trial ended without that, it was rated 1-2, or you chose Remove. Restorable for 30 days by default, then the file is deleted and the track is never proposed again

Each list has a search, a sort and a filter, and the counter beside Manage opens it. Tracks are checked every 10 minutes, right after a merge or Start clean of your history, and when the rules change, even with every mix off.

The app only ever moves or deletes files in the trial folder and its trash, at /data/trial_trash/.

Limits

All of these are in Manage:

Refresh RateDaily Mix or Daily Mix+ every 24 hours, Discover every 7 days
Length30 tracks in each mix, and 30 in Discover
New tracks percent33% of each Daily Mix+. Unheard tracks on trial take these places first, so at most 40 tracks are downloaded per refresh with 4 mixes of 30
Disk LimitDownloads stop at 100 unheard tracks or 20 GB in quarantine, and Activity says so. These two count every profile together, as they share the disk

Trial rules

Under Versions → Trial in Manage:

Automatic validationOn by default. Off, nothing moves by itself: tracks stay on trial until you keep or remove them, and only the trash still empties
Days on trial30 by default. A new length also applies to tracks already there
Days in the trash30 by default, then the file is deleted
Delete permanently after the trial period endsOff by default. On, a track that did not earn its place is deleted at once instead of going to the trash
Bypass user reviewOff by default. On, a track that earned its place is kept at once
Plays to validate a track2 by default, counted from the day it was downloaded
Starred or rated 4-5 in Navidrome countsOn by default. A rating of 1-2 then also ends the trial early

A track already under To review stays there if you make a rule stricter.

When something looks wrong

  • No mix appears. Fix missing metadata may still be running: see Manage, or use Regenerate now. A library or listening history with too little in it gives fewer mixes, or none.
  • A track in the wrong mix. Genres come from your file tags and Deezer, and a wrong tag misleads the mix.
  • "Unmapped path" in Activity. Add the folder in Settings → Navidrome paths. The downloads folder needs a mapping too.
  • Plays do not keep a track. Check that plays reach the app, under Stats → Recent plays.
  • Stars and ratings do nothing. Navidrome's database must be mounted at /import/navidrome, and Starred or rated 4-5 in Navidrome counts must be on. They are read at most once an hour.
  • "Connect a Deezer account". Daily Mix+ and Discover need one to download.
