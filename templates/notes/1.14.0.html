Turn on playlists built from what you listen to, under Made for you on the Dashboard. Everything is off until you choose Manage.

  • Daily Mix. Up to 4 mixes a day, only with music you already have, each around a track, an album or an artist that keeps coming back in your listening. Seeds change every day.
  • Daily Mix+. The same idea, with a share of tracks you do not have yet, a third by default. You choose Off, Daily Mix or Daily Mix+ with one switch.
  • Discover. Every week, only new releases and tracks you never heard, chosen from your habits.
  • How mixes choose. A track mix looks for the same tempo and genre, an album mix also favors the artists on the album, and an artist mix adds what you barely played of that artist.
  • Fix missing metadata. Finds the tempo and genre your files lack and adds them to their tags, in Your library or Settings → Library index. Tempo is measured from the audio, genre comes from Deezer. Existing values are kept. A FLAC with room in its header only has that header rewritten, saved first so a brutal stop can put it back, and its audio is not touched. Other files are written on a copy that replaces them only once its audio bytes are found identical. A file that cannot take tags keeps its results in the app, and Undo metadata fix in Manage takes back what was added. New downloads now carry their genre too.
  • Activity. Each task shows its elapsed time, speed and time left, and its log says when each step happened. Long tasks can now be stopped with Cancel. Buttons waiting for a second click now stand out.
  • Quarantine. What these playlists download stays on trial for 30 days. Play it twice, or star it or rate it 4 or 5 in Navidrome, and it is kept. Otherwise it waits under To review, and nothing leaves without your decision.
  • Trash. A removed track waits 30 days in the trash, then its file is deleted and it is never proposed again.

Before you turn it on

  • Nothing touches your files until you click Fix missing metadata or turn on a mix. From then on, new files are handled on their own.
  • The first pass takes a while: it reads and tags each file, and Navidrome then reads them again at its next scan. Settings → Performance sets how many files are handled at once. Mixes start once most of it is done.
  • Your music folders must be mounted writable for tags to be added. Otherwise the results still count for the mixes, writing is tried again every day, and Manage tells you.
  • Stars and ratings need Navidrome's database mounted at /import/navidrome, as for the history import. Without it, only plays count.
  • The Docker image is about 110 MB larger, for the tempo measure.
