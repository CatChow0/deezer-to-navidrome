The quarantine of Made for you is rebuilt: lists you can search and sort, and rules you choose.

  • Lists you can sort. On trial, To review and Trash now have a search, a sort and a filter, and each track shows its cover and the playlist that brought it. The tabs come in that order, with To review in green and Trash in red.
  • A counter on the Dashboard. Beside Manage, three cells count the tracks on trial, to review and in the trash. A click opens that list.
  • To review changes meaning. It now holds the tracks that earned their place, waiting for you to confirm them, with Keep all for the tracks shown. A track that does not earn its place by the end of its trial goes to the trash, where it can still be restored.
  • Your rules. A new Trial section under Versions: automatic validation on or off, days on trial and in the trash, deleting for good at the end of the trial, keeping without review, the number of plays needed, and whether stars and ratings count.
  • Plays from a merge count at once. Tracks on trial were only looked at when mixes were built. They are now checked every 10 minutes, right after a merge or Start clean of your history, and as soon as the rules change, even with every mix off.
  • Empty trash. The trash can be emptied in one go.

After the update

  • Tracks already under To review are sorted once by the new rules: those that earned their place stay, the others go to the trash, restorable for 30 days.
  • Stars and ratings are read from Navidrome at most once an hour.
