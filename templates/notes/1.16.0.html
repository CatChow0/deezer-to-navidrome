The app now works for several Navidrome users and several Deezer accounts: each playlist goes to the users you choose, as their own playlist, and each user follows the releases and mixes of their own Deezer account. Each user also has their own listening history, recaps and Made for you.

  • Navidrome accounts. Settings → Navidrome accounts lists the Navidrome users this app works for. Add one with the address of your Navidrome, a user name and its password, test the connection, edit it, make it the default, or remove it.
  • Your install becomes the first account. It keeps working exactly as before, with its .m3u8 playlists, history and mixes. Add its address and password when you want it to be ready for per-user playlists.
  • Profiles. Profile, at the top of the page, chooses the Navidrome user this browser works for: the dashboard shows that user's playlists. Another browser, or the phone, keeps its own choice.
  • In step with Navidrome. A playlist of the app that its user deletes in Navidrome leaves that profile, and the app if no other profile has it; one renamed there keeps its new name, shown in the dashboard. Checked every 10 minutes and when the dashboard opens.
  • Playlists made by hand. Also in Navidrome shows, read-only, the playlists the user made in Navidrome, with their cover and tracks.
  • Playlists for each user. A playlist goes to the accounts ticked under In Navidrome in its drawer. For each one, it is published as that user's own private playlist through Navidrome's API, kept up to date in place, and tracks Navidrome has not scanned yet are added as soon as it has.
  • Your playlists stay as they are until you choose. Your install's account keeps writing .m3u8 files, which Navidrome gives to its first admin. Publish as this user… in Settings → Navidrome accounts moves them to the API: the app publishes them, then removes its .m3u8 files and the copies Navidrome had imported from them. If Navidrome refuses one, nothing changes.
  • Per account in the dashboard. Playlists added go to the account of this browser, and the dashboard shows that account's playlists. Adding a playlist another account already has shares it. Delete takes a shared playlist away from this account only.
  • Deleting removes it from Navidrome. A deleted playlist now also leaves Navidrome: the playlist this app made there, or its .m3u8 file. Navidrome keeps a playlist it imported even once the file is gone, so that copy is removed too when the account has its password; otherwise the app says to delete it in Navidrome.
  • Several Deezer accounts. Settings → Deezer accounts replaces the single account. Add one with its ARL, checked by signing in to Deezer, then edit it, check it again, make it the default, or remove it.
  • One account for the downloads. The account marked Use for download fetches the files for everyone, the default one when none is marked. The line under the list says which one, and in which format.
  • Releases and mixes per profile. Each Navidrome account chooses its Deezer account in its form, the default one otherwise. The Releases tab, its muted artists and Choose mixes follow it, and a mix of another account is read with that account. CatBoard keeps reading the default profile's feed.
  • A history for each user. Each Navidrome account has its own listening history, recaps and token: the Stats tab follows the profile chosen at the top of the page. Settings → Listening stats shows the token of that profile, to paste in Navidrome by its user.
  • Imports read each user's plays. A rebuild takes only the plays of the profile's Navidrome user, where it used to take everyone's. Maloja's plays stay your install's.
  • Made for you for each user. Daily Mix, Daily Mix+ and Discover are built from each user's own listening history, Discover from the releases of their Deezer account, and published for them only. Manage shows the settings and the quarantine of the profile chosen at the top of the page.
  • A shared quarantine. A track has one file and a verdict from each user who got it, decided with their own plays and stars: another user's stars no longer count. A mix can take a track another user has on trial without downloading it again. Keep puts it in the library for everyone; a refusal only counts for its user, and the file goes to the trash once nobody has it on trial. A file another user's playlist relies on goes to the library rather than the trash. A track on trial that is also downloaded by hand no longer leaves a second copy: the library's copy takes its place. The disk limits stay common to everyone, since the disk is.
  • Removing a Navidrome account takes its playlists out of Navidrome and of the app (a playlist shared with other accounts stays for them), sends its tracks in quarantine to your install's account, and keeps its history aside.
  • Encrypted credentials. Passwords are kept encrypted, and so are the secrets already saved in secrets.json (Deezer ARLs, tokens). None of them is ever shown again or written in a log.

After the update

  • For stronger protection, set D2N_ENCRYPTION_KEY to a long random string. Without it, the key is kept in /data/encryption.key, so a copy of the whole data folder carries it along.
  • Going back to an older version means entering your Deezer ARL and tokens again: older versions cannot read the encrypted values.
  • Navidrome does not accept API keys yet: sign accounts in with their password.
  • Publishing through the API finds each track by its file path in Navidrome's database, which must be mounted at /import/navidrome, as for the history import. Without it, tracks are searched by their tags, which misses some.
  • The app replaces the tracks of its playlists in Navidrome at each update: tracks changed there are lost at the next one. A new name or a deletion made there is kept.
  • If every playlist of the app vanishes at once from a Navidrome user, the app takes it as a reset of Navidrome and publishes them again rather than removing them.
  • Your Deezer account becomes the first Deezer account, the default one. The premium account you had for downloads becomes a second account, marked for the downloads. DEEZER_ARL and DEEZER_ARL_PREMIUM keep working: their ARL is changed there, not in Settings.
  • Your listening history stays in stats.db, unchanged: it is your install's account's. Your token keeps working. For another Navidrome user, choose their profile, make their token in Settings → Listening stats, and link ListenBrainz in Navidrome as that user.
  • Your mixes, settings and quarantine stay as they were: they are your install's account's. mixes.db is copied to /data/backups/pre-1.16.0/ before its quarantine table changes. Another user's Made for you starts off: turn it on in Manage with their profile chosen.
  • Start clean now keeps only your own Navidrome user's plays: if someone else's plays were imported into your history before, it takes them out.
  • Your saved playlists and secrets.json are first copied to /data/backups/pre-1.16.0/.
