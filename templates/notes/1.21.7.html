Fixes found by a code audit.

  • Playlists. Opening a playlist, or acting on it, no longer waits for Deezer: a missing title or cover is only looked up on the dashboard and by the scheduler. A cover link that does not answer is tried again an hour later instead of on each page.
  • Activity. Listing the tasks no longer holds up the other tasks while it checks what you may see: Activity and the Android app stay quick even when Deezer is slow.
  • Update. Pull the new image: there is nothing to change in your settings.
