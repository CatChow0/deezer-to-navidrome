Your Deezer playlists, private ones too, added in one click from the Dashboard.

From Deezer

  • Your playlists. Playlists → From Deezer lists the playlists of the profile's Deezer account: Loved tracks, those you made and your favorites, private ones too, with their size. Add one and it appears on the Dashboard, read with that account, however long it is.
  • One panel, three tabs. Your playlists, Mixes (the Flow and the mixes Deezer makes for you) and Recommended (the playlists Deezer suggests). Choose mixes opens it on the mixes.
  • Links still work. A Spotify link, a public Deezer playlist of someone else, or a CSV file.
  • Android app. The same three tabs; Add a playlist starts with From Deezer.
