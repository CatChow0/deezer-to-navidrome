Your Deezer playlists, private ones too, added in one click from the Dashboard.

From Deezer

  • Your playlists. Playlists → From Deezer lists the playlists of the profile's Deezer account: Loved tracks, those you made and your favorites, private ones too, with their size. Add one and it appears on the Dashboard, read with that account, however long it is.
  • One panel, three tabs. Your playlists, Mixes (the Flow and the mixes Deezer makes for you) and Recommended (the playlists Deezer suggests).
  • Mixes with your playlists. The Mixes section is gone: the mixes you follow sit in Playlists, marked Mix (in the Android app too, out of Made for you).
  • Links still work. A Spotify link, a public Deezer playlist of someone else, or a CSV file.
  • Android app. The same three tabs; Add a playlist starts with From Deezer.

Fixed

  • Covers of your own playlists. A Deezer playlist added through the account showed Deezer's grey placeholder when its cover was an album's. It shows its cover now, and those already added ask Deezer for theirs again at the first start.
  • Downloads with sign-in off. With DISABLE_AUTH and no account in the app, Use for download was refused since 1.20.0: it chooses the downloads account again.
