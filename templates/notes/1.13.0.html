A new Stats tab shows what you listen to. It replaces Wrapped, Maloja and lb-enricher if you ran them.

  • Story recaps. A full-screen story for each month and year, built from what stood out in that period: a song played on repeat, your longest sitting, who rose and fell, and a listener personality. Slides move on by themselves; tap to skip, hold or press Space to pause.
  • Your own music. Recaps play your files, starting about a third of the way into each song. A track with no playable file uses Deezer's 30-second preview.
  • Charts for any period. Top artists, albums and tracks by week, month or year, with genres, when you listen, and a searchable list of your plays. Open an artist, album or track to see its plays over time.
  • Covers kept here. Artist, album and track covers come from your files first, then Deezer, and are stored in the data folder.
  • Your own history. The app stores your plays itself, and can import the past ones from Navidrome and Maloja. Stats → Your history shows it and rebuilds it.
  • Settings reorganised. Three groups instead of a long column, and the save bar only appears when there is something to save.
  • New and Help. This panel keeps the notes of each version. Help is now a full guide with a search.

Start recording your plays

  1. Import your history (optional). Mount the folders holding navidrome.db and, if you have it, malojadb.sqlite read-only at /import/navidrome and /import/maloja, then choose Rebuild (merge) under Stats → Your history.
  2. Point Navidrome at this app. Create a token in Settings → Listening stats. Set ND_LISTENBRAINZ_BASEURL=http://deezer-to-navidrome:8080/apis/listenbrainz/1/ in Navidrome, restart it, and link ListenBrainz in its personal settings using that token.
  3. Check that plays arrive. Play a track in Navidrome and find it under Stats → Recent plays. Run Rebuild (merge) once more to pick up what you played during the switch: Navidrome kept it in its own history.
  4. Remove what you no longer need. Stop and remove lb-enricher, Maloja and Wrapped. Keep the read-only Navidrome mount: Start clean needs it, and Rebuild (merge) reads Navidrome's new plays through it. The app keeps its own copy of Maloja's database.
