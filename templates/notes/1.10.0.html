This app now downloads from Deezer by itself. The separate kmille2/deezer-downloader container is no longer needed.

  • Download anything from the app. A track, a whole album, or an artist's discography, from search, a playlist, or the Releases view. Files are tagged with the cover included.
  • Quality follows your account. Premium downloads FLAC, a free account MP3 128 kbps. With a free account, add the arl of a premium one in Settings for the downloads only: your mixes keep using the first.
  • New setting. Settings → Performance → Download threads sets how many downloads run at the same time.

If you ran deezer-downloader

Your setup ran two containers, and this app sent its downloads through DEEZER_DL_BASE_URL. To remove the second one:

  • delete the whole deezer-downloader service, with its own volumes and settings;
  • in deezer-to-navidrome, delete DEEZER_DL_BASE_URL: it is no longer read;
  • mount your downloads folder in deezer-to-navidrome, writable;
  • paste the arl you gave deezer-downloader in Settings → Deezer account.

Lines in red go away, lines in green are new. Keep the rest of your file as it is. Albums still go in albums/Artist - Album/ and single tracks in songs/, so your Navidrome paths and playlists keep working.
