The Android app now does what the web interface does: your playlists and the search, Made for you, Stats, Releases and Settings, on your phone.

  • Playlists and search. Add a playlist by link, ID or CSV, or share a Deezer or Spotify link to the app. Scan it, download what is missing, and fix a track by choosing a library file or a Deezer match. Search Deezer, play previews, download a track, an album or an artist's albums.
  • Made for you. The Daily Mix mode, Discover, the limits and trial rules, and the quarantine: swipe right to keep a track, left to remove it.
  • Stats. Tops with their covers, the genres and hours charts, artist, album and track pages, your plays, and the recaps played as stories.
  • Releases and Settings. The feed of your Deezer account, muting artists, and every section of Settings, with a confirmation before anything is deleted.
  • Without the server. The app shows the pages it saved, for the same profile only, and waits for the server before changing anything. The server now marks its answers per profile so a phone never mixes two of them.
  • Large fonts and TalkBack. Every screen was checked at 130 % and 200 % text size and with TalkBack.
  • Faster. The app is smaller (3.6 MB instead of 11.5 MB) and compiles its most used code on install, so it starts faster from the first launch.
  • Update. The app offers this version from your server, as for the last one.
