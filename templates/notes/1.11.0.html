• Explicit stays explicit. Explicit tracks and albums are marked E, and you get exactly the version you clicked. When a release is not available in your account's country, the app may take another release of the same recording, but never the censored one in place of the explicit one.
  • Your account's country. Settings → Deezer account shows the country next to the account badge: Deezer serves the catalogue of that country, which decides what can be downloaded.
  • A help window. A short guide, opened from the top bar, with live checks of your Deezer account and downloads folder.
