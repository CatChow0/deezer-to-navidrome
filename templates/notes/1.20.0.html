Several accounts in the app, each with its own profiles: a Super-Admin, Admins and Standard accounts, and Settings in spaces.

Accounts

  • Three privileges. The account made at the first start is the Super-Admin, never removed. An Admin manages the Standard accounts and the server's settings; only the Super-Admin makes an Admin Standard again. A Standard account has its own profiles, never the server's settings.
  • Settings → Users (Admins) adds an account with a temporary password, chosen again by its user at the first sign-in. It edits an account's username, privilege and password, and removes one, giving its Navidrome and Deezer accounts to another account or removing them with it.
  • Your own profiles. Each account has its Navidrome accounts, Deezer accounts, downloads account, API token, CatBoard webhook and devices. The picker shows your own profiles only, and a playlist is reached only from a profile it is on. A Navidrome user belongs to one account of the app.
  • Sharing a Deezer account. Share lets other accounts use one of yours for their profiles and downloads; they never see its ARL.
  • CatBoard. Choose the profiles your token reads: /api/releases answers their feeds as one, so CatBoard needs no change.

Forgot your password

  • Recovery codes. Each account has one, shown once: at the first sign-in, then each time it is made again in Sign-in and other apps, by an Admin for a Standard, or by the Super-Admin for an Admin.
  • Forgot your password? on the sign-in page takes the username, the code and a new password. The Super-Admin without its code can have a one-time code written in recovery-code.txt in the data folder, valid 15 minutes.

Settings

  • Four spaces. General (Admins), Accounts, Stats and sign-in and Users (Admins), with Maintenance under each.
  • Maintenance. Rebuild from scratch, applying duplicates, Made for you's disk limits and Reset data are for the Admins. Clear your profiles clears the match results, covers or playlists of your own profiles only.

From 1.19 and the Android app

  • Nothing to redo. Your account becomes the Super-Admin and keeps every profile, Deezer account, token and webhook; your devices stay signed in.
  • Android app. The same spaces in Settings, Users for the Admins, sharing a Deezer account, Forgot your password and choosing your password at the first sign-in.
