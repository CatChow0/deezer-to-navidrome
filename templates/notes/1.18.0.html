The Android app: the web interface on your phone, starting with signing in, the profiles and Activity. Install it from Settings; it then updates itself from your server.

  • The app. Sign in with your account and pick the profile at the top, as on this page. Activity follows the tasks live and stops one. More shows the versions and signs the phone out.
  • Settings → Android app. Downloads the app built with this version of the server.
  • Updates. When the server is newer, the app offers its version and installs it over the old one, keeping its sign-in and its profile.
  • Next. Playlists, search, Made for you, Stats, Releases and Settings come to the app in the next versions.

After the update

  • On your phone, allow installs from your browser for the first install, and from D2N for the updates.
  • Behind Authelia or another login proxy, let /api/v1/ through if it is not done yet: the app cannot pass a login page.
