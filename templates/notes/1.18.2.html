Fixes for artists who share a name, Deezer accounts with a private profile, and big Deezer playlists.

  • Private Deezer profiles. The release feed stopped with "This user's profile is private" when the account had "Make my profile private" checked in Deezer. It now reads the favorite artists with the account's own session: the profile can stay private.
  • Private Deezer playlists. A private playlist of your own can be added and scanned: it is read with the Deezer account of your profile.
  • Playlists over 400 tracks. Deezer playlists stopped at their 400th track, without a word: the scan, the missing tracks and the playlist in Navidrome now hold all of them. Scan a big playlist again to get the rest.
  • Artists who share a name. Made for you could propose tracks of another artist with the same name as one you listen to (another PLK than the French rapper). The artist is now the one credited on the tracks you play, never a search by name. Tracks of a namesake already on trial go to the trash, with "Another artist with the same name": restore one from there if it was a good find.
  • Update. The Android app offers this version from your server; nothing changes in it.
